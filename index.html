Frequently Asked Questions

Where is Kashmir Sinthan Top located? +
We are based in Dessu, Daksum, Anantnag, Jammu & Kashmir. A government registered agency running tours to Sinthan Top, Dal Lake, Sonamarg, Pahalgam, Gulmarg and Ladakh.
How do I book a tour package? +
Simply WhatsApp us at +91 9797125922 or fill the contact form. We respond within 2 hours with a custom itinerary. No upfront payment needed for a quote.
What is Sinthan Top and how high is it? +
Sinthan Top is a stunning high mountain pass in Anantnag district at approximately 12,000 feet (3,800m) above sea level, offering panoramic Himalayan views. Less crowded and more scenic than most Kashmir spots.
Do you provide car rental services? +
Yes! We offer Innova Crysta, SUVs, Tempo Travellers and luxury vehicles for self-driven or chauffeur-driven tours across Kashmir. See our Cars page for options.
What is the best time to visit Kashmir? +
March–May: Spring & tulip gardens. June–August: Summer meadows & Sinthan Top. September–November: Golden chinar autumn. December–February: Gulmarg skiing. Every season is beautiful!
