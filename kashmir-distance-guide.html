Kashmir's tourist circuit spans roughly 130 km end-to-end between Srinagar and Sinthan Top, with Pahalgam, Gulmarg and Sonamarg each under 2.5 hours from Srinagar by road. Longer routes — Gurez Valley, Kishtwar, and Leh via Zojila — cross seasonal mountain passes that close in winter. This guide lists drive distances and durations for 20 of the most-traveled routes, maintained by Kashmir Sinthan Top Tour & Travel, a government-registered operator based in Daksum, Anantnag.

Last updated: July 2026 · Compiled from local driver route data

Whether you're planning a short Srinagar–Pahalgam loop or a longer circuit through Anantnag to Gurez Valley, knowing real drive times — not just straight-line km — matters in Kashmir's mountain terrain. Road conditions, pass closures, and elevation change the "effective distance" more than the number on a map. ✦ Frequently Asked ✦

Distance Questions

How far is Srinagar from Pahalgam? +
Srinagar to Pahalgam is approximately 90 km, a scenic drive of about 2.5 hours on a well-paved road.
How far is Anantnag from Gurez Valley? +
Anantnag to Gurez Valley is approximately 180 km, roughly a 6-hour drive routed via Srinagar and Bandipora over the Razdan Pass.
How far is Sinthan Top from Srinagar? +
Srinagar to Sinthan Top is approximately 130 km, taking about 4 hours. The pass is open roughly June to October.
Can Gurez Valley be visited in one day from Srinagar? +
Technically yes at 4-5 hours each way via Razdan Pass, but it's not recommended as a single-day trip — most travelers stay overnight.
How far is Srinagar from Leh? +
Approximately 434 km via the Zojila Pass. Most travelers split this into a two-day drive with an overnight stop in Kargil or Dras.
How far is Kishtwar from Sinthan Top? +
Kishtwar to Sinthan Top is approximately 85 km, roughly a 3-hour drive on the same mountain pass road, open only in the summer/autumn months.
How far is Sinthan Top from Kokernag? +
Kokernag to Sinthan Top is approximately 53 km, about a 2-hour drive via Daksum on a seasonal mountain road.
How far is Sinthan Top from Jammu? +
Jammu to Sinthan Top is approximately 280 km, around 9-10 hours via Anantnag. Most travelers split this into two days.
Is Sadhna Top the same as Sinthan Top? +
No — they're two different passes. Sadhna Top is near Kupwara/Tangdhar (~90 km from Srinagar), while Sinthan Top is near Anantnag/Daksum (~130 km from Srinagar).
