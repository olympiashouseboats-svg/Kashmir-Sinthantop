Who We Are

Kashmir Sinthan Top
Tour & Travel

We are a government registered tour and travel agency proudly based in Dessu, Daksum, Anantnag — at the very foothills of the Himalayan range, minutes from Sinthan Top itself.

Founded by locals who grew up exploring these mountains, our mission is simple: share the real Kashmir — its untouched landscapes, warm hospitality and hidden treasures — with travelers from across India and the world.

We are not just a travel company. We are storytellers, guides, photographers and mountain-lovers who have spent years perfecting the art of crafting unforgettable Himalayan experiences.
