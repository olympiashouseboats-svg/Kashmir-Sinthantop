Athena Houseboat is the sister vessel to Olympias — part of the same Olympias & Athena heritage group at olympiasathena.com — and earns its own place in our top two for a distinct set of reasons. Where Olympias commands with drama and panorama, Athena enchants with warmth and intimacy. The walnut wood interiors — particularly the dining room ceiling panels — are among the finest examples of traditional Kashmiri woodcraft we have encountered on Dal Lake across many years of partnerships.

Athena's slightly sheltered position gives it a garden-like quality — lotus and willows frame the views, and the surrounding water is noticeably quieter than the open lake in the evenings. Returning guests often specifically request Athena for the peacefulness of its particular corner of Dal Lake.

For honeymoon couples, Athena is frequently our first suggestion over Olympias — the intimate scale and warmer interiors create a romantic atmosphere that the slightly larger Olympias can't quite match. For families who want their own complete vessel, Athena is perfect. Book directly at olympiasathena.com.

How to Reach Olympias & Athena from Srinagar Airport

Srinagar International Airport (Sheikh ul Alam Airport) is approximately 14 km from Dal Lake — a 30–45 minute drive depending on traffic. We include airport pickup in all our Kashmir packages and deliver guests to the lakeside ghat (dock). From there, your private shikara ferries you across to Olympias or Athena — an arrival experience that starts the houseboat stay perfectly.

For standalone houseboat bookings via olympiasathena.com, the houseboat team can coordinate airport pickup on request, or pre-book a registered taxi from the airport.
